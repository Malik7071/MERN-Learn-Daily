Which One Should You Pick?

A 10-second gut check before you commit.
Choose SQL if you need…
Strict data integrity & safe transactions
Complex relationships & reporting
A mature, well-documented ecosystem
Choose NoSQL if you need…
Massive scale & flexible, evolving data
Rapid iteration on your data model
High-speed reads & writes at scale
Pro tip: many production systems use both — a pattern called polyglot persistence.
